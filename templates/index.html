How do I get my details from LinkedIn?

Option A – Profile PDF (quickest)

  1. Open your profile on linkedin.com (desktop browser).
  2. Click the More (or Resources) button under your name.
  3. Choose Save to PDF. The file downloads automatically.
  4. Upload that .pdf below.

Option B – Full data export (most complete)

  1. Go to Me › Settings & Privacy › Data privacy › Get a copy of your data.
  2. Pick Want something in particular? and tick Profile, Positions, Education, Skills, Certifications, Projects (or choose the larger archive).
  3. Click Request archive. LinkedIn emails you a download link (can take a few minutes to 24 hours).
  4. Download the .zip and upload it below — no need to unzip it.

The PDF is read by pattern-matching, so please review the filled fields before generating.

LinkedIn doesn't allow reading a profile from its URL (scraping breaks its Terms), so the URL is only added to your resume.
